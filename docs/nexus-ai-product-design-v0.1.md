# Nexus AI Product Design v0.1

## Vision

Nexus AI is an AI collaboration platform powered by specialized Atlas Agents.

Slogan:

> Connect ideas. Create possibilities.

## Core Idea

Nexus AI does not replace human decisions. It helps users understand problems, explore possibilities, make decisions, and move toward execution.

## Architecture

Nexus Core

↓

Atlas Framework

↓

Specialized Atlas Agents

## Current Direction

First application:

Project Atlas

Idea → Execution Agent

Future agents:

- Research Atlas
- Writing Atlas
- Evidence Atlas

## Development Philosophy

Build a lightweight, extensible AI system with a focus on Agent collaboration, memory, tools, and continuous evolution.
